# 05: Put the slate to Melissa

**What to build:** Melissa hears the four surviving names and picks one. This is the decision the whole naming ticket has been waiting on, and it is the only part of it a machine cannot do.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately — but it gates 06 and 07).

**Status:** ready-for-human

**The slate:** **Nearabouts**, **Wellnigh**, **Thereish**, **Fairish**.

Each has passed the ownability gate in ADR-0008: no live mark in Nice 9/28/41 anywhere, no existing game of that name in any store, an unclaimed `<name> game` search query, a domain form available, and spellable on hearing. Nothing she is shown is a name that would have to be taken away later.

**How to run it.** Say them **cold** — aloud, in the sentence *"want to play ___?"*, with no case made for any of them. Her unprimed reaction is the instrument; explaining why one is better is how it gets lost. Then let her play a Page, and say them again.

Two of the four are on the slate as instruments rather than as likely winners: `Thereish` is the only pure coinage, so it tests whether she wants a word that exists; `Fairish` is the only one carrying an echo of `Fair Enough`, so it tests whether her objection was to that phrase or to that whole direction. Both are informative when they lose.

- [ ] Melissa has played at least one Page
- [ ] The four names have been said aloud cold, before she played
- [ ] The four names have been said aloud again after she played
- [ ] Her pick is recorded, along with both readings and whether they agreed
- [ ] If the cold pick and the post-Page pick disagree, that is written down as the finding it is — falsifier 2 in ADR-0008 — rather than resolved by picking one
