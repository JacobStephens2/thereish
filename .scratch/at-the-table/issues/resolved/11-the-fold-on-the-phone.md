## Answer

1. **Phase-dependent layout**: In `MeaningGameWeb.RoomLive`, the speaker bar and Code card sections were extracted into private function components (`speaker_bar/1` and `room_code/1`).
   - In `:waiting` and `:giving` (or if `@room` is nil), they render at the top of the main container above forms, preserving the existing behavior for joining and giving.
   - From `:writing` on (`:writing`, `:bridging`, `:assaying`, `:reveal`, `:closed`), they render after the play controls and immediately before "In the Room".
   - A phone with no Seat mid-Page follows the same rule: the join form appears first, followed by the speaker bar and Code card above "In the Room".
2. **LiveView tests**: Updated `test/meaning_game_web/live/room_live_test.exs` with explicit position assertions (`:binary.match/2`):
   - In `:waiting`, speaker bar and Code card are above the join form.
   - In `:giving`, speaker bar and Code card are above the setting form.

   - In `:bridging`, Attempt line, Bookends, and Bridge form are above speaker bar and Code card, which are before "In the Room".
