# Full table: one Bookend per Seat, on by default

ADR-0010 grades every Bridge as one set and carries the two furthest apart forward as the next Attempt's Bookends; ADR-0011 has the Author write two Bookends from two of the Seats' Gives. Two nights of play said the game was too easy — a couple sometimes melded on the second Attempt — and that the game's sweet spot may be more than two players. Decision (2026-09-11): a Room has a rule called **Full table**, on by default, under which the count of Bookends equals the count of Seats for the whole Page. The Author draws one Bookend from every Give, and after a miss every Seat's Bridge carries forward, so a six-Seat Room bridges six words. Off, the Room plays the two-Bookend rule exactly as before. The rule is a Room fact beside quips and music, flipped from any phone or the Stage with no settings page, and a Page keeps the rule it was written under. Grading does not change: the Assayer already reads the set as one Closeness and still names the furthest pair, which the Chronicle keeps and the two-Bookend rule uses. Why one per Give rather than a wider pair from two Gives: every player sees a word of their own on the table, and that sense of contribution is the point as much as the difficulty. Why a switch rather than a rule: two Bookends give a phone-only table focus and an easy layout, and Jacob wants to play both.

Sides were already retired (ADR-0015), so this rule is one table. The group as a whole is the game.

## Considered options

- **A settings page.** Rejected for now: a page holding one switch is a pass-through; the Room-facts row already exists on phone and Stage. Build the page the day there are three or more rules.
- **Two independent switches**, one for the opening width and one for the carry-forward. Rejected: they are one idea, and two switches invite a mixed state nobody asked for.
- **A cap on Bookends below the seat cap.** Rejected: the seat cap of six is already what a phone can show, and six short words fit.
- **Attributing first-Attempt Bookends to their Gives on the table.** Rejected: Gives are secret until the first reveal, and a Mask beside a Bookend would leak which world each Give came from. Carried Bookends, whose authors are known, do show their Seat's Mask from the second Attempt on.
- **Keeping the old Assayer prompt for two-Bookend Attempts.** Rejected: one prompt version that reads N Bookends; the orphaned Precedents are cheap to rebuild.

## Consequences

Amendment (2026-09-12, [issue #126](https://github.com/JacobStephens2/thereish/issues/126)): Rooms must accommodate eight Seats. The six-Seat layout assumption above no longer sets the capacity; Full table still means one Bookend per Seat. Room rules move into a settings modal as the controls have grown, with Voice and a simple sound control remaining immediately available.

The glossary's Bookends become "two or more", and Full table is defined. The Author's interface gains one fact, the count of Bookends wanted, and its gate checks that count. The Assayer's prompt bumps (assay-4) to read N Bookends, orphaning cached Precedents once; the Precedent key already treats Bookends and Bridges as sets, so N of each needs nothing more. Two-Seat play is unchanged by this rule; two-Seat difficulty is issue #115. Whether phones should hide the Bookends under a Stage is filed separately.
