# The machine's roles answer to a Steward, not a Keeper

"Keeper" was proposed as the umbrella for the Author, the Assayer and the Scribe and then used as though settled across seven documents and `lib/`, while the design brief still listed it as parked. Decision (2026-09-04): **Steward**. The three roles are people with jobs, so their umbrella should be a person with a job rather than a name for the seam itself — which ruled out *the Seam*, *the Door* and *the Oracle* — and *Steward* says "manages others" more plainly than *Keeper* while dropping its goalkeeper and dungeon-keeper noise. The tiebreaker was room to grow: **Steward is one letter from Stewart**, so if the stage view ever gets a Jackbox-style host, the character already has a name and a job. Taken now rather than later because this is the only rename in the naming pass that touches `lib/`, and `lib/` grows every day Stage 1 runs.
