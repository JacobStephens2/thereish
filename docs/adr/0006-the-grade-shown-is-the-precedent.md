# The grade the players saw is the precedent

The Keeper session proposed that borderline pairs (60–85) and every overrule get a stronger second opinion from Opus 5 written into the Chronicle as the precedent, even when the live beat showed the fast model's grade. Decision (2026-09-02): **no silent rewrites.** The verdict cache stores exactly what the players saw; a second opinion becomes a Chronicle *annotation* — the Scribe's "on reflection" — that feeds rubric tuning offline and never changes what the cache returns. The only thing that rewrites precedent is a joint player overrule (design law 3), which is a story, not a correction. Why: design law 1 — a ruling that quietly differs from the one shown last time is the self-contradiction players never forgive.
