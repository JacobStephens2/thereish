# Keep Vamps with their Pages

Vamps were disposable while Tracks were retained, which prevents a player reviewing a Page from identifying or replaying the temporary music they heard. Decision (2026-09-12, [issue #126](https://github.com/JacobStephens2/thereish/issues/126)): retain generated Vamps with their Pages and make them playable in the Chronicle, accepting the additional media storage to preserve that part of the play history. Retention does not make a Vamp a Repertoire member, and unrelated Repertoire playback need not be recorded against the Page.
