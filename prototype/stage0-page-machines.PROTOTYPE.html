PROTOTYPE — the two Page machines

Throwaway Stage 0 logic demo. Nothing here ships.

The question this answers: before any phones, servers or real models exist — do the rules of the two prototype candidates hold up when you push them through real cases? Is a Page of Chronicle Clover (clue-binding across word pairs, with an authored decoy instead of a blind-drawn one) and a Page of Mind-Meld (both of you secretly bridge two anchor words; failed bridges become the next anchors) a shape worth building for two people on a couch?

What is fake here: the Author and the Assayer are stubs with a fixed word bank and a toy scoring heuristic — but they keep the real contracts: pre-generated Pages, a schema-shaped verdict, a fixed 2.5 s reveal beat, both orderings graded, every verdict cached (same inputs → same ruling, forever), reasoning always shown, and a joint overrule the Chronicle records. Judge the rules and the feel of the beat, not the word choices.

Simplifications: one word per card instead of four (so the clover ring is clickable); both seats shown on one screen, so nothing is truly private yet; memory lives only as long as this tab is open.
